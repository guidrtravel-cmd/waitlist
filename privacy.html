Privacy

Short version: we only collect what we need to build Guidr and to tell you when it launches. We never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Your quiz answers (things like how you travel and plan trips).
  • Basic info your browser sends: language, time zone, and which link brought you here.
  • A scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address to stop spam bots. It is deleted after 24 hours.

Why

  • To build a travel app people actually want (we look at the answers as a group).
  • To email you once when Guidr launches, with your free month code.

What we don’t do

  • We never sell or rent your data.
  • No ads, no tracking pixels, no spam.

Where it’s stored

Securely on Supabase servers in the EU.

Delete your data

Want your email and answers removed? Message us on X at @guidrtravel or reply to any email from us, and we’ll delete everything.
